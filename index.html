Why human art is worth protecting

It means something

A human chooses every line for a reason. That intent is what you feel in a piece, and what a generated image can only imitate.

It carries a story

Your brief meets someone’s life and craft. The result is one of a kind, made with you, not averaged from millions of images.

It keeps artists alive

If hiring humans stops paying, human art disappears. Fair pay is how we keep it.

The problem

Artists undercharge

Clients compare hand-made work with instant images. Artists guess their prices, lose hours and burn out.

Clients can’t tell what is fair

Quotes vary wildly, so hiring a human feels risky and expensive. Many skip it.
